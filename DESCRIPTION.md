# Tarbound

**Summary (one line, for the project card):**
Low-tech storage and autocrafting for NeoForge 1.21.1. Pour tar, tune a haunted radio, and let bound spirits run your machines.

---

Tarbound is a storage and automation mod for people who like the idea of Refined Storage or AE2 but don't want cables, power grids or a tech tree. Everything runs on black tar, an old radio that hears your chests, and the spirits you bind to your machines.

## What it does

**Pour tar, see everything.** Every chest, barrel or storage block touching a body of tar joins one shared pool. Put a Scrying Font (a haunted radio) next to the tar and you can search, take, insert and craft from all of it in one screen.

**Fluids too.** Tanks touching the tar join the pool. Fill buckets from the radio, pour them back in, and contracts pump fluid straight into your machines.

**Transient Antennas and stored power.** Pull finished items, fluids and power back from machines anywhere in the dimension, or push them out. The radio holds energy as Essence of the Void, a starry fluid you can bucket and keep in tanks.

**Antennas for the hard-to-reach spots.** Can't run tar somewhere? Tune a Tar Antenna to your radio and stick it on a chest. Stack it up to three high, from rabbit ears to a full cell tower, for more range, and chain antennas to reach across your base. Each antenna has a mode: normal, read-only for output chests, or write-only for feed and void chests. You can also set a priority.

**Reliquaries run your machines.** Set a skull-and-candle Reliquary on top of a furnace, a Create millstone or any modded machine, and it shows up in the radio slot by slot. Furnaces under one stay lit without fuel, and finished items drain back into your storage on their own.

**Contracts for autocrafting.** Write a Spirit Contract from any JEI or EMI recipe at the Scribing Lectern, give it to a Reliquary, then order what you need from the radio. A Reliquary on a crafting table becomes a crafting station that crafts straight from storage, and more stations split the work. Standing contracts keep a machine fed with anything in a tag, like "smelt every raw ore".

**Feed the spirits.** Storage is always free, but the spirits doing your automation want candles. Right-click the radio with them, hopper them in, or keep a stack in storage and let the radio help itself. Tallow drops from cows and pigs, so candles are easy to farm, and there's a plant wax option if you have Create.

**Weakened Blazes.** Scorch a blaze spawner with flint and steel and it starts spawning Weakened Blazes, which drop the Weakened Souls you need for tar. No spawner handy? Grind soul sand or blaze burners in Create, or craft souls from soul sand and a flint and steel.

**Spirit Board.** Open your radio from anywhere in the dimension.

## Plays well with

- **JEI and EMI**: recipe filling, contract writing, and a button to keep the radio's search box in sync with the viewer's.
- **Create**: Ponder scenes that walk you through everything, plant wax mixing, chutes and belts kept out of the pool, and it works with item vaults.
- **Jade**: extra info on the radio, Reliquaries and antennas.

None of these are required.

## Good to know

- NeoForge 1.21.1. Needed on both client and server.
- Orders plan their own sub-crafts: missing ingredients are made first by your other contracts, while a Shade (a floating ghost head) holds the order's stuff so nothing else takes it.
- Almost everything is adjustable in the server config, including turning the candle costs off completely.
- Hold W over any Tarbound item with Create (or Ponder) installed to see how it works.
- Tar seeps form in swamps, badlands and deserts. Tar spreads like lava, poisons mobs (never players), and mobs that die in it feed your radio.
- The Ossuary: every Reliquary's contracts on one screen, from anywhere.
- Abandoned researchers' cabins hide in forests, with old antennas, loot and field notes.
- Tar-soaked logs and planks carry tar like the fluid does, so you can hide your links under the floor.
- Prefer a blockier look? Turn on "Tarbound: Classic Cubes" in the Resource Packs screen. It comes with the mod: a square radio, simple antennas and flatter item icons.
